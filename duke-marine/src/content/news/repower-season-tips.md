Repowering a skiff or panga is one of the best investments you can make in your
time on the water — but a little planning goes a long way. Before you commit to a
new outboard, our service bench recommends checking these five things.

## 1. Transom height & shaft length

A mismatched shaft length hurts performance and can cause ventilation. We measure
your transom and confirm the right 20" or 25" shaft before ordering.

## 2. Weight & capacity

More horsepower usually means more weight. We make sure your hull is rated for the
engine so you stay safe and legal.

## 3. Propping for how you actually run

Loaded for a dive charter runs very differently from a light flats skiff. We help
you choose pitch and material for your typical load.

## 4. Rigging & controls

Cables, gauges and controls may need updating with a new engine. We handle the
full rig-up in-house and test everything before you leave.

## 5. Service & parts support

Buy from a shop that stocks the filters, impellers and service parts you'll need
all season. That's the difference between a quick fix and a lost weekend.
