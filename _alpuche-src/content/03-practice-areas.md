# Practice areas

The question that brings you to a lawyer is often practical. Is the person selling the property able to transfer it? What should a shareholder agreement say? What options are available when a payment is overdue or an agreement breaks down? Start with the area closest to your concern. The office can help you find the right starting point if your matter crosses more than one.

## Property and business

### Real Estate

Buying, selling or transferring real estate in Belize can involve a review of title, registered interests, transaction terms and documents needed for transfer. We assist with those steps and can identify matters that call for further enquiries before closing.

[Real Estate](/practice-areas/real-estate/)

### Corporate & Commercial

We advise on local and cross-border commercial arrangements, company and partnership structures, shareholder agreements, joint ventures and transactions involving Belize. We can assist with formation and discuss where an authorised provider is required.

[Corporate & Commercial](/practice-areas/corporate-commercial/)

### Banking, Finance, Tax & Regulatory

We advise on Belize financing documentation, including facility agreements, guarantees, mortgages and charges, as well as Belize tax and regulatory matters. A single transaction may raise questions about signing authority, security, licensing and cross-border payments.

[Banking, Finance, Tax & Regulatory](/practice-areas/banking-finance-tax-regulatory/)

### Intellectual Property

We assist with registration of trademarks and patents and advise on copyright protection. Common starting points include a new brand, an invention ahead of disclosure, and ownership questions involving work created by employees or contractors.

[Intellectual Property](/practice-areas/intellectual-property/)

## Disputes and personal matters

### Dispute Resolution

The practice advises and represents clients in Belize court proceedings, arbitration and mediation. The appropriate route depends on the agreement, the evidence, timing and what a workable outcome would look like.

[Dispute Resolution](/practice-areas/dispute-resolution/)

### Family Law

We advise and represent clients on divorce, adoptions and other family matters. If a situation involves more than one country or an existing court order, raise that with the office at the start.

[Family Law](/practice-areas/family-law/)

### Qualified Retired Persons Programme

We advise people considering Belize’s retirement programme and help them understand the questions to resolve before applying or making commitments about property, shipping or relocation.

[Qualified Retired Persons Programme](/practice-areas/qualified-retired-persons/)
