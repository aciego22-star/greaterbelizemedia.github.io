Services

Services and departments

Seven clinical service categories, supported by clear patient information and easy ways to reach us.

Clinical Care

Our Clinical Services

General descriptions of each category. Details, schedules and availability are being confirmed with the hospital.

Emergency Services

Care for urgent medical needs, supported by on-site imaging, laboratory and surgical capability. Availability and hours are being confirmed with the hospital.

Specialists

Consultations with specialist physicians. A directory of specialists and their schedules will be published once confirmed by the hospital.

Laboratory

On-site clinical laboratory testing that supports diagnosis, treatment and follow-up care.

CT Scan

CT imaging available for emergencies, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Images may be sent to your physician through WhatsApp or email.

Operating days and the image delivery process are subject to hospital confirmation.

X-Ray

Radiography for bones, chest and other common imaging needs, read in coordination with your treating physician.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound studies, including prenatal and abdominal imaging, performed in a calm, private setting.

Surgery

Surgical procedures performed in modern, well-equipped theatres, with pre-operative assessment arranged through specialist consultation.
