For Buyers

Looking to source Belizean seafood?

Whether you are exploring products for distribution, hospitality, food service or another commercial requirement, send your enquiry and the team can follow up regarding current products and availability.

Before you write

What helps the team answer quickly

A short, specific enquiry gets a faster and more useful reply. If you can, include the product you are interested in, the market you are supplying, an indicative volume and your timeline.

  • The product or products you are interested in
  • The country or market you are supplying
  • An indicative volume and how often you would need it
  • Your timeline and any documentation your market requires

This form is an enquiry pathway. It is not an ordering, pricing or inventory system, and sending it does not place an order.
