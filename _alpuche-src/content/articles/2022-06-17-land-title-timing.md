# How long does a land title take in Belize?

Obtaining a land title in Belize can take over a year.

## When you become the beneficial owner

Once you have lodged your transfer form, paid your stamp duty and paid for your property, you are the beneficial owner and should be able to take possession of it.

For the steps that come before closing, see the [guide to buying property in Belize](/insights/buying-property-in-belize/).
