Quality & Responsible Fisheries

Careful handling and better information

How the cooperative works with its product, its records and the wider management of Belize's fisheries.

Quality

Handling that protects the product

National Fishermen has a long-standing focus on processing, quality control and market access.

Seafood is only as good as the care it receives between the water and the buyer. Consistent handling, grading and cold-chain discipline are what let a small fishing nation compete on quality.

Current certifications and permitted food-safety wording are confirmed directly with the cooperative.

Traceability

Knowing where the catch came from

National has participated in electronic catch-documentation and traceability initiatives designed to improve operational efficiency and seafood information through the supply chain.

Good catch information helps the cooperative run more efficiently and helps buyers understand what they are purchasing. It is a practical tool as much as a compliance one.

Source: FishWise, the story of the National Fishermen's Cooperative in Belize.

Responsible Fisheries

Part of Belize's fisheries management effort

National has participated in Belize's spiny lobster Fishery Improvement Project and in broader fisheries-management efforts.

A Fishery Improvement Project brings industry, government and non-governmental partners together to work on the same fishery over time. For a cooperative whose members depend on that fishery, the interest is direct.

Source: FishSource, Belize spiny lobster FIP.

A note on claims

What this page does not say

National Fishermen's participation in traceability work and in the spiny lobster Fishery Improvement Project is a matter of public record. That is not the same as saying every product is certified sustainable or fully traceable today. For current certifications and the standards that apply to a specific product, contact the team.
