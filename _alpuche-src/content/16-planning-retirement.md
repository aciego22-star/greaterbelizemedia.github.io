# Planning retirement in Belize

People often begin with a preferred town or a date to move. Use those as planning goals and work backwards. Ask which parts of the plan depend on an application, which involve separate legal work and which commitments you can keep flexible until the position is clearer.

## Plan the status and the move separately

The [Belize Tourism Board](https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program/) publishes current guidance on the Qualified Retirement Program, commonly called the QRP. Its criteria, documentation and conditions apply to the programme. If you plan to work, operate a business, include family members or rely on a particular tax outcome, raise those plans with counsel before you make commitments.

Build in time for records that must come from elsewhere. Identity, family and income documents can take time to assemble and may need certification. Before ordering documents or paying for translations, check the current guidance and ask which records are relevant to your application.

## Decide whether to rent or buy first

Renting initially can give you time to understand a community before buying. If you already have a property in mind, discuss the title, proposed agreement and how the purchase fits with your move. A transaction with overseas parties or funds can raise signing, payment and exchange-control questions that are separate from the programme.

Ask what the purchase agreement requires if plans change, including what happens to any deposit and when the parties must close. The same planning principle applies to a long lease or a shipment of personal goods.

## Check conditions before booking a shipment

If you are considering importing household goods or a vehicle, check the current rules and timing before signing a shipping agreement or buying something specifically for import. Ask what approvals or documents you may need and whether there are conditions tied to a programme benefit. Plan for the possibility that shipping and application timelines will differ.

## Decide what to raise with the firm

- Who expects to move and where they currently live.
- Whether you are considering QRP status or comparing other options.
- Whether you plan to rent, buy, work or run a business.
- Which travel, housing or shipping commitments are already in place.
- The dates that matter most and where you still have flexibility.

For general planning information only. Current programme requirements and any decision on an application rest with the relevant authorities.
