Preparing for Your Hospital Visit

A short, practical guide to making your appointment at Orange Walk Multicare Hospital as smooth as possible.

Author: awaiting hospital approval Date: awaiting publication

A hospital visit is easier when you arrive prepared. A few minutes of organisation before leaving home can save you time at registration and help your care team understand your situation better.

Before your visit

Confirm the date, time and service of your appointment. If your physician gave you a referral, keep it together with your identification so you can present both when you arrive. If you have previous studies or results related to the reason for your visit, bring those as well.

  • Valid identification
  • Medical or NHI referral, if applicable
  • A list of current medications, with doses if you know them
  • Results or images from previous studies
  • Your questions, written down in advance

On the day of the appointment

Plan to arrive a few minutes before your scheduled time. This allows time for registration and for any instructions staff may need to give you. Wear comfortable clothing; for some imaging studies it can help to avoid jewellery or metal objects.

If you cannot attend, let the hospital know in advance by calling +501 675-2328, so your slot can be offered to someone else.

During your consultation

Describe your symptoms or reason for the visit in your own words, and mention any known allergies or conditions. Do not hesitate to ask for instructions to be repeated or explained; understanding your care plan is part of a good consultation.

After the visit

Before you leave, make sure you know three things: what the next steps are, when you should return, and who to call if you have questions. If studies were ordered, ask how and when you will receive the results.

This article is general guidance and does not replace medical advice. In an emergency, call Orange Walk Multicare Hospital at +501 675-2328.
