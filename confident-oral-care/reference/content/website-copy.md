## Your First Visit

### Start with what you’d like to talk about

When you request a visit, choose a general or orthodontic consultation if that is the kind of appointment you’re looking for. If you’re unsure what to choose, the team can help you find the appropriate starting point.

### Bring the questions that matter to you

Write down anything you would like to cover, from a dental concern to questions about comfort, timing, or fees. If you have previous X-rays, records, or an appliance you want checked, call the practice ahead of time to ask what to bring.

### Talk through the next step

The dentist can explain the findings from your visit, whether more information is needed, and what follow-up may be appropriate. You can ask which decisions can wait and what to expect at a future appointment.
