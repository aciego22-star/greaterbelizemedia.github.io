Who We Are

Serious, Personal Care for Northern Belize

Orange Walk Multicare Hospital is a private hospital located in Orange Walk, Belize. We bring emergency care, specialist consultations, surgery, laboratory testing and diagnostic imaging together in one modern facility.

Our goal is simple: families in northern Belize should be able to receive quality care without traveling far from home.

Professional. Modern. Accessible.
