# Website and AI assistant notice

The website provides general information about the practice and about legal topics involving Belize. Seek advice from an attorney about your own matter. Rules, procedures and agency requirements may change.

## Contacting the firm

Sending an enquiry, using the website assistant or receiving a reply about availability does not establish an attorney–client relationship. The firm must confirm that it has accepted a matter and agreed the terms of engagement. If a legal deadline or hearing is approaching, call the office promptly. Do not use the website or assistant to serve documents or notify the firm of a deadline.

## Using the website assistant

The website assistant is automated. It can help you find a practice area or public office contact information. It cannot review your case, calculate a deadline, accept instructions, confirm an appointment or give legal advice. Information shared with the website assistant should be limited to general questions. Please keep confidential details, identification numbers, financial records and documents out of the chat.

## External information

Links to official registries, regulators and other organisations are provided for general reference. Those organisations maintain their own information and requirements.
