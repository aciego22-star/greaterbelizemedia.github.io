Our Cooperative

Owned by the fishers it was built for

Founded in 1966, National Fishermen Producers' Co-operative Society Ltd. is a Belizean fisher-owned cooperative. Its members form the foundation of the organization and elect the managing committee that oversees the Society.

The cooperative model helps members combine their efforts in producing, processing, marketing, distributing and selling fisheries products. What one fisher cannot reach alone, the Society can reach together.

Our Fishers

Behind every product is a fishing community

National Fishermen was built around the cooperative model, creating a collective structure through which Belizean fishers can connect their work at sea with processing and market opportunities.

Membership is the foundation of the Society. Fishers who join become part owners, and they elect the managing committee that governs the organization on their behalf.

Quality, Traceability & Responsible Fisheries

Built on careful handling and good information

Three areas that shape how the cooperative works with its members, its product and its buyers.

Quality

National Fishermen has a long-standing focus on processing, quality control and market access. Careful handling from landing through preparation is what makes the product worth buying.

Traceability

National has participated in electronic catch-documentation and traceability initiatives designed to improve operational efficiency and seafood information through the supply chain.

Responsible Fisheries

National has participated in Belize's spiny lobster Fishery Improvement Project and in broader fisheries-management efforts alongside sector partners.
