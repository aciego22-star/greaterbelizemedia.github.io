Proposed Future Capability

OWM AI

A proposed digital assistant that could help visitors find services, request appointments and reach the right person. Presented for discussion; not an approved or operational hospital system.

The Idea

Simple Help, With Clear Limits

If approved by the hospital in a future phase, OWM AI could handle practical questions on the website, always using hospital-approved information.

Find Services

It could help patients find hospital services.

Appointments

It could assist with appointment requests.

Directions and Contact

It could provide directions and contact information.

General Questions

It could answer approved general hospital questions.

Patient Information

It could help visitors navigate patient information.

Escalate to People

It would escalate to hospital staff when human assistance is required.

What OWM AI Would Not Do

  • It would not diagnose medical conditions.
  • It would not replace doctors, nurses or hospital staff.
  • It would not use information the hospital has not approved.
  • It would not handle emergencies; those concerns must be directed to hospital staff.
Next Step

A Decision for the Hospital

OWM AI is a concept for a future phase. It would only be built if the hospital approves it, with content reviewed and limits defined by the hospital itself.
