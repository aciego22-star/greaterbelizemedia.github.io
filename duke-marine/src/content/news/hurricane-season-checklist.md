Storm season is a fact of life on the Belizean coast. A few hours of preparation
now can save your vessel, and your season. Use this checklist and stop by if you
need to top up any gear.

## Safety & signalling

- Inspect life jackets, flares and fire extinguishers; replace anything expired.
- Check that your EPIRB is registered and the battery is in date.
- Refresh your first-aid kit.

## Ground tackle & lines

- Upsize anchors and add a second rode for storm mooring.
- Inspect dock lines and fenders for chafe; replace worn line.
- Carry extra shackles and chafe guards.

## Vessel & documents

- Service bilge pumps and check float switches.
- Photograph your vessel and serial numbers for insurance.
- Keep registration and insurance documents somewhere dry and portable.
