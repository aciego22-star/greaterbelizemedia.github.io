# Qualified Retired Persons Programme in Belize

A move to Belize is a series of decisions about where to live, how to support yourself and which commitments to make before you arrive. If you are considering the Qualified Retired Persons Programme, an early discussion can help you plan those decisions around the application and any conditions that may apply.

The Belize Tourism Board administers the programme and refers to it as the Qualified Retirement Program, or QRP. The firm advises prospective applicants on the legal questions raised by the programme and on related matters such as property purchases. Janine Ebanks-Alpuche previously administered the QRP at the Belize Tourism Board and now manages the firm’s practice.

## How we can help

**Understand the programme in the context of your plans.** Discuss who is planning to move, where they currently live, what income they expect to rely on and whether family members may need to be included. The [Belize Tourism Board’s QRP guidance](https://www.belizetourismboard.org/programs-events/retirement-program/) is the starting point for current criteria and documentation.

**Plan the application sequence.** Different records may need to be obtained from different authorities or institutions, and some have timing or certification requirements. Build time into your plans for records about identity, family relationships, background and income where relevant. Ask the office to confirm a suitable channel for sharing documents.

**Connect relocation and real estate decisions.** Decide whether you plan to rent first or buy, and discuss how your property plans relate to the move. A purchase deserves its own title and transaction review. If you are negotiating from overseas, raise your proposed signing arrangements and the movement of funds early.

**Review plans for personal goods or business activity.** The programme publishes conditions relating to possible benefits and ongoing obligations. If you are considering shipping goods, importing a vehicle, working or carrying on business, raise those plans before making commitments. Permissions and treatment can depend on the activity and the relevant rules.

## What the Belize Tourism Board assesses

The BTB’s published criteria cover age, eligible applicants and dependants, evidence of retirement income from outside Belize, a deposit undertaking, security clearance and time spent in Belize. Its guidance also sets out renewal steps and conditions for specific benefits. These are separate questions to review against your plans. When planning a move, budget for the application, document preparation and renewals as well as travel and housing. Confirm current requirements and charges with the BTB before relying on them.

## Questions to consider before committing to a move

Identify your preferred timing, who will move with you, whether you plan to work or invest, and what financial or travel arrangements you will need. If you are looking at property, decide which commitments can wait until the legal and programme questions are clearer. Keep a list of what you need to confirm with the firm, the Belize Tourism Board, and advisers in your home country where appropriate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will I qualify for the QRP?

The Belize Tourism Board assesses applications under its current requirements. The firm can discuss the criteria in light of your plans and identify questions that need to be confirmed. Call the office to discuss your circumstances.

### Does the QRP settle every residency, employment or tax question?

The programme has its own status and conditions. If you have separate questions about permanent residency, permission to work or tax residence, raise them directly and ask what additional advice or referrals may be needed.

### Can I include my family?

The programme provides for eligible dependants under its published conditions. Discuss who plans to move and check the current requirements before relying on a particular application plan.

### Should I buy property or ship belongings before applying?

Discuss the application timeline and any separate purchase or import conditions before committing funds. The order of those decisions can affect cost and flexibility if plans change.
