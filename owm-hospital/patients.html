Patient information

Everything you need to plan a visit to Orange Walk Multicare Hospital, from what to bring to how NHI referrals work.

Do not use the appointment form for a medical emergency. For urgent assistance, call Orange Walk Multicare Hospital directly at +501 675-2328.

General List

General Items to Bring

  • A valid identification document
  • Your medical or NHI referral, if you have one
  • A list of the medications you currently take
  • Previous results or studies related to your visit
  • Your questions written down, so nothing is forgotten

This is general guidance; the hospital will confirm the specific requirements of each service.

Referrals

Referral Information

If your physician referred you to us for a study or consultation, bring the written referral to your appointment. Our team will coordinate results with your treating physician.

NHI

National Health Insurance (NHI) Referrals

Orange Walk Multicare Hospital now accepts NHI referrals for registered persons in the Orange Walk and Corozal districts. Present your NHI referral and identification when you arrive.

Eligibility and the services covered by NHI are determined by the NHI programme and are subject to confirmation. Our staff can guide you, but cannot guarantee coverage.

Appointments

Appointment Requests

Send an online request with the service you need and your preferred date. Our team will contact you to confirm. A request is not a confirmed appointment.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request an appointment?

Use the online form or call +501 675-2328. Our team will confirm the date and time with you.

Do you accept NHI referrals?

Yes, for registered persons in the Orange Walk and Corozal districts. Eligibility and coverage are determined by the NHI programme.

Where is the hospital located?

At Mile 56.5 Philip Goldson Highway, Orange Walk, Belize.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Identification, any referral, a list of your medications and previous studies related to your visit.

How do I receive my imaging results?

Results are coordinated with your treating physician. For CT studies, images may be sent through WhatsApp or email; the exact process is subject to hospital confirmation.
