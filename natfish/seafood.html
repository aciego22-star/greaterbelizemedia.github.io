Our Seafood

Seafood from Belizean waters

Lobster and conch have long been associated with the cooperative's fishing, processing and market activity.

Product

Lobster

Caribbean spiny lobster is a longstanding product associated with National Fishermen's fishing, processing and market activity.

Lobster is also the focus of Belize's spiny lobster Fishery Improvement Project, in which National Fishermen has participated alongside sector partners.

Enquire about lobster
Product

Conch

Queen conch is a longstanding product associated with National Fishermen's fishing, processing and market activity.

Conch requires careful cleaning and handling between landing and market. That handling is part of what the cooperative structure was built to support.
