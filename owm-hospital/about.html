About OWM Hospital

A trusted healthcare partner for our community — built on compassion, expertise, and a commitment to every patient.

Our Story

Serving our community for over 15 years.

OWM Hospital was founded with a simple belief: everyone deserves access to quality healthcare, delivered with dignity and compassion. What began as a small clinic has grown into a full-service hospital serving thousands of patients every year.

Today, our team of doctors, nurses, and support staff continues that mission — combining modern medicine with the warmth of a community hospital that knows its patients by name.
