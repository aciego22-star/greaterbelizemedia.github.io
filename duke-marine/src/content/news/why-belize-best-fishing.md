Ask anglers who have fished around the world where they would go back tomorrow,
and a lot of them will say Belize. This small country on the Caribbean coast of
Central America packs more world-class fishing into a short run from shore than
almost anywhere else. Whether you are chasing your first bonefish or your
hundredth permit, Belize gives you the kind of day on the water you remember for
years. Here is why, and how Duke Marine helps you get the most out of it.

## A grand slam within reach

Belize is famous for the flats grand slam, landing a bonefish, a permit and a
tarpon in a single day. Very few places on earth make that realistic, and Belize
is one of them. The country sits behind the second largest barrier reef in the
world, and that reef creates a huge system of shallow flats, lagoons, channels
and mangrove creeks. Bonefish move across the sand in big numbers, permit tail
on the turtle grass, and tarpon roll in the channels and river mouths. You can
sight fish all of it in clear, warm water that stays fishable most of the year.

## Fishing for every style

The variety is what keeps people coming back. On the flats you can wade or pole
a skiff and cast to fish you can actually see. Along the reef you will find
snapper, grouper, jacks and barracuda. Head to the blue water past the reef and
you are into the range of mackerel, wahoo, tuna and the occasional billfish. In
the rivers and estuaries, snook and tarpon give you a completely different fight.
One trip can cover skinny water in the morning and deeper water in the afternoon.

## Warm water and a long season

Belize fishes well throughout the year. Bonefish and barracuda are around in
good numbers all season. Permit fishing stays strong for most of the year, while
tarpon peak in the warmer months as the migratory fish move through. Water
temperatures stay warm, the trade winds are manageable on most days, and Belize
City puts you within easy reach of the northern cayes, the atolls and the
southern lagoons. You are never far from the fish.

## Local knowledge makes the difference

Great fishing is about more than a great location. It is about matching your gear
to the water and the target species. A permit leader that is too heavy will spook
fish on a calm flat. The wrong fly line or the wrong soft plastic can turn a good
day slow. The guides and captains who fish Belize every day know exactly what
works here, and that local knowledge is worth more than any catalog.
