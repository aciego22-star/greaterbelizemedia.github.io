How it works

  1. 1

    Enter your details

    Your name, email address, the invoice number and the amount to pay. Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.

  2. 2

    Pay on Belize Bank’s secure page

    You are then redirected to the Belize Bank Payment Gateway to enter your credit or debit card details. Card details are entered only on Belize Bank Limited’s secure system; this website never sees or stores them.

  3. 3

    Confirmation and receipt

    After a successful payment you will see a confirmation on screen and receive a payment receipt for your records.
