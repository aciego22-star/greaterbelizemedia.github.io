# Cosmic Pharmacy Logo Pack

Production-ready reconstruction based on Cosmic Pharmacy's supplied logo.

## Recommended website use

- **Dark cosmic hero/header:** `svg/cosmic-pharmacy-logo-white.svg`
- **White or very light sections:** `svg/cosmic-pharmacy-logo-primary.svg`
- **Single-colour documents or print:** `svg/cosmic-pharmacy-logo-navy.svg`
- **Favicon, mobile shortcut and compact UI:** assets in `web/`
- **Facebook, Instagram and TikTok profile image:** `png/cosmic-pharmacy-social-avatar-1024.png`

Use SVG wherever possible. It remains sharp at every screen size. PNG files are supplied as transparent fallbacks and social-media assets.

## Rules

- Keep the wording exactly as supplied: **COSMIC PHARMACY** and **MEDICINE·HEALTH·BEAUTY**.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, recolour or add shadows, bevels or outlines.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the width of the small diamond in the symbol.
- Do not place the primary blue/pink logo over a busy image. Use the white version on the dark cosmic hero.
- Do not use the full horizontal logo below approximately 240 px wide; switch to the standalone icon.
