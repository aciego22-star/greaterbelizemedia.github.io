When the only shareholder of a Belize company dies

By José M. Alpuche, Attorney-at-Law · 20 June 2022

If a shareholder of a Belize company dies, the shares cannot be transferred until the deceased person’s estate is probated.

Why this matters for a one-person company

If that person was the sole shareholder and the sole director, the company may be unable to conduct business until the probate process is complete. Probate can take between four and twelve months.

A step to consider now

If your company has a sole shareholder and a sole director, consider at least appointing additional directors who would be able to carry on managing the business should something happen to you.
