How it works

How an order request works

  1. Choose your seafood

    Select from NATFISH’s approved product list and tell NATFISH AI the approximate quantity you need.

  2. Share your details

    Provide your name, telephone or WhatsApp number, email address and any useful pickup or fulfilment notes.

  3. The NATFISH team confirms

    Your request is sent to the team, who will confirm availability, pricing and the next steps for pickup or fulfilment.

Starting an order is conversational. NATFISH AI guides you through the details, summarizes the request and asks permission before it is shared with the team.

Using it well

Your information, handled with care

NATFISH AI is an AI-powered service. It uses the information you provide to answer your questions and help prepare your request. When contact or order details need to be shared with the NATFISH team, NATFISH AI should ask for your permission first.

Share only the information needed for your enquiry or order request. Payment arrangements and final order details are handled with the NATFISH team after the request is reviewed.

  • The visitor is clearly told that NATFISH AI is an AI service.
  • Only information relevant to the conversation or request should be collected.
  • Consent is requested before contact or order details are sent to the team.
  • The visitor may contact NATFISH directly instead of using AI.
