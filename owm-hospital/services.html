Services & Departments

Comprehensive medical care for every stage of life — delivered by teams who put patients first.

🚑 Emergency & Trauma Care

Immediate treatment for accidents, injuries, and life-threatening conditions. Rapid triage, resuscitation bays, and ambulance coordination.

Open 24 hours, 7 days a week

🩺 General Medicine & Outpatient Clinic

Consultations, diagnosis, and treatment for everyday illnesses and chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and asthma.

Mon–Fri 8:00am–5:00pm · Sat 8:00am–12:00pm

🤰 Maternity & Obstetrics

Prenatal check-ups, safe labor and delivery, cesarean capability, and postnatal care for mother and baby.

Deliveries 24/7 · Prenatal clinic Mon–Fri 8:00am–4:00pm

🧒 Pediatrics

Well-child visits, immunizations, and treatment of childhood illness from newborns through adolescence.

Mon–Fri 8:00am–5:00pm

🏥 Surgery

General and minor surgical procedures, pre-operative assessment, and dedicated post-operative recovery care.

Scheduled procedures · Emergency surgery 24/7

🔬 Laboratory Services

Blood work, urinalysis, microbiology, and diagnostic testing with fast, reliable results.

Mon–Sat 7:00am–6:00pm · Emergency lab 24/7

📷 Imaging & Radiology

X-ray and ultrasound services supporting accurate diagnosis across all departments.

Mon–Fri 8:00am–5:00pm · Emergency imaging 24/7

💊 Pharmacy

On-site pharmacy stocking prescribed medications, with guidance from our licensed pharmacists.

Mon–Sat 8:00am–6:00pm

🦷 Dental Clinic

Cleanings, fillings, extractions, and preventive dental care for adults and children.

Mon–Fri 9:00am–4:00pm (by appointment)

❤️ Specialist Clinics

Visiting specialists in cardiology, gynecology, orthopedics, and more. Ask about the current clinic calendar.
