# After launch: rumpopo.com

The website side is done: every page tells search engines its official address is
`https://rumpopo.com/…` (English) and `https://rumpopo.com/es/…` (Spanish), with a
bilingual sitemap, structured data, an FAQ page that answer engines can quote, and
`llms.txt` for AI assistants. The old address `shiny-truffle-ef9459.netlify.app`
redirects to rumpopo.com.

These steps need the owners' accounts, so only they can do them.

## This week (most important)

1. **Google Search Console** (free): <https://search.google.com/search-console>
   - Add a *Domain* property for `rumpopo.com` and verify it with the TXT record Google
     gives you, at the company where the domain was bought.
   - Sitemaps → submit `https://rumpopo.com/sitemap.xml`.
   - URL inspection → enter `https://rumpopo.com/` → *Request indexing*. Repeat for
     `https://rumpopo.com/flavours/` and `https://rumpopo.com/es/`.
2. **Google Business Profile** (free): <https://business.google.com>
   - The biggest single boost for “rumpopo Belize City” searches and Google Maps.
   - Name: Cojac’s Sumptuous Rumpopo. Website: `https://rumpopo.com`. Phone/WhatsApp:
     +501 621-8372. Add the bottle and gallery photos. If customers do not visit Diego St,
     set it up as a *service-area business* (Belize City, delivery) and hide the address.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (free): <https://www.bing.com/webmasters> → *Import from Google
   Search Console*. Bing also feeds ChatGPT and Copilot search.
4. **Point every profile to the site**: Facebook page *Website* field and Instagram bio link →
   `https://rumpopo.com`. Pin a post announcing the new website and the Rumpopo Slush.

## Next few weeks (authority and AI visibility)

## How to check it is working

In Search Console after 1 to 3 weeks: *Pages* (how many are indexed; expect 24), *Performance*
(impressions, clicks and the searches people used, such as “rumpopo”, “rum popo Belize”), and
*Enhancements* (FAQ and breadcrumbs). No cookies or tracking on the site are needed for this.
