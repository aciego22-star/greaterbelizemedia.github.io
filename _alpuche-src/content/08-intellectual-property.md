# Intellectual property law in Belize

A name, design, invention or creative work can hold much of a business’s value. The legal questions are often practical. Who owns the logo a contractor designed? Does a company registration protect a brand? What should an inventor do before showing a prototype to someone else?

The firm assists with registration of trademarks and patents and advises on copyright protection in Belize. Start with the asset, how you intend to use it and where you need protection.

## How we can help

**Trademarks.** Discuss what should be protected, who should own it and the goods or services the mark will cover. A word mark and a logo can present different filing questions. Early discussion is particularly useful before committing to packaging, signage, a domain or a public launch.

**Patents.** Discuss whether a patent filing deserves consideration and where you may want protection. If an invention is still private, tell the office about planned presentations or disclosures in general terms and ask for a suitable channel before sharing technical details.

**Copyright.** Discuss ownership and use of original creative work, including questions arising from commissioning, employment or licensing. The [Belize Intellectual Property Office](https://www.belipo.bz/copyright/) describes copyright protection as arising automatically on creation and offers a deposit facility. An attorney can review the work, the agreement and the issue you need to resolve.

**Protection beyond Belize.** If your business already sells elsewhere or plans to expand, make a list of the markets that matter. Registration and enforcement questions can differ by country. Discuss which work the firm can handle in Belize and where advisers elsewhere may be needed.

## Where BELIPO fits

The [Belize Intellectual Property Office](https://www.belipo.bz/) is the national intellectual property registry. It publishes information about [trademark](https://www.belipo.bz/trademarks/) and [patent](https://www.belipo.bz/patents/) applications. The legal work begins with identifying the asset and the proposed owner. A filing may follow if it fits the client’s objectives. The registry determines the outcome of applications it examines.

## Protecting IP as part of a wider transaction

A business may acquire a brand with a purchase, license a logo for use by a distributor or commission new material for a launch. In each case, identify the asset precisely and discuss who can use it, where, for how long and under what terms. If the party providing the asset commissioned it from someone else, ask counsel to review the ownership records and agreements that come with it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is registering a company or business name the same as registering a trademark?

They serve different purposes. Company or business-name registration deals with the business record. Trademark registration addresses protection of a mark in connection with goods or services. Discuss both if the brand is important to your venture.

### Does a foreign trademark cover Belize?

Protection depends on the registrations or designations in place. Share the jurisdiction and general nature of the existing protection with the office and ask whether a Belize filing or review may be needed.

### Can I send my invention details through the website?

Call the office first. Describe the kind of assistance you are seeking and mention any planned disclosure date. The office can explain how to provide sensitive material if a review is arranged.

### What if another business is already using a similar name?

Preserve what you have seen and note when you found it. The firm can discuss whether the issue raises a registration, ownership or dispute question. Avoid drawing conclusions about infringement from a name alone.
