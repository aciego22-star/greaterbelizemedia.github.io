# ICB website rebuild

The site lives in `icb-concept/`. A rebuild of ICB's website, commissioned
by ICB, going live on `www.icbinsurance.com` and reviewed in the meantime
on the agency subdomain.

**Read `icb-concept/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md` first.** It records what the build
is waiting on from ICB, what was assumed in the meantime, and what changes
when each answer arrives.

`icb-concept/DEPLOY.md` covers building, hosting, the domain cutover and
the launch-day switch.
